## 2. Detailed Experiment Records

### Experiment EXP-001: Community Pain Discovery & ATS Failure Mining
- **Hypothesis:** Indian freshers experience repeated off-campus rejection primarily driven by unparseable multi-column Canva resumes, generic project descriptions, and misunderstanding of Naukri's algorithmic search filters.
- **Action:** Mined community discussions across `r/developersIndia`, `r/Indian_Academia`, Quora, and LinkedIn hiring forums. Analyzed feedback on paid services (e.g., Naukri FastForward) and common resume review threads.
- **Result:**
  - High recurrence of students reporting 300+ applications with zero callbacks.
  - Consistent evidence of Canva 2-column templates and skill rating bars ("Java: 80%") being ridiculed by tech reviewers and garbled by standard ATS parsers.
  - Widespread dissatisfaction with paid services (₹1,500–₹5,000) citing generic rewrites and persistent tele-calling.
  - Heavy reliance on outdated biodata formats mandated by college placement cells (father's name, declarations, photos).
- **Learning:** The pain is acute, visceral, and widely discussed, but students are skeptical of paid resume services due to poor prior experiences. A 100% free, high-utility toolkit with zero sales pressure is the optimal wedge to build trust.
- **Next Action:** Scope a lean, 7-component Free MVP addressing these exact bottlenecks without feature bloat (Execute EXP-002).

### Experiment EXP-002: Free MVP Scoping & Architecture Specification
- **Hypothesis:** By scoping an MVP focused strictly on single-column ATS compatibility, biodata removal, JD keyword extraction, and Naukri profile ranking, we can solve 90% of a fresher's immediate technical application hurdles without overwhelming them with unnecessary interview guides or complex software.
- **Action:** Authored `RESEARCH.md`, `PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, `VALIDATION_PLAN.md`, and `CONTENT_PLAN.md`. Established strict quality rules (zero fake data, free software compatibility, clearly marked demonstration examples).
- **Result:** Complete specifications compiled for 7 core tools (Template in .docx/.pdf, 2 Checklists, 1 JD Worksheet, 1 Naukri Guide, 1 Project Bullet Formula, 1 Tracker).
- **Learning:** Keeping the MVP focused strictly on the resume-to-interview-call funnel prevents scope creep and respects the ₹0 budget.
- **Next Action:** Submit baseline documentation to Founder (Rohit) for review and formal approval before creating template assets.
