## 1. Editorial Philosophy & Standards

To build enduring brand equity among Indian students and freshers, every piece of published content must adhere to four strict quality standards:

1. **High Standalone Utility (Zero-Click Value):**
   - The reader must learn a concrete, immediately applicable skill from the post alone—even if they never click the link to download the free kit.
2. **Deconstruct the "Why" (Show the Machinery):**
   - Don't just tell candidates: *"Don't use Canva."* Show how an ATS parser extracts text out of order from a two-column PDF and why a recruiter's screen ends up blank.
3. **No Deceptive Clickbait:**
   - Prohibited headlines: *"Do this ONE TRICK to get a ₹20 LPA job!"*, *"Guaranteed 100% ATS score!"*
   - Approved headlines: *"Why two-column resumes break in ATS parsers (and how to test yours in 30 seconds)"*, *"5 pieces of college biodata cluttering your fresher resume"*.
4. **Natural, Unforced Discovery:**
   - Never plead with readers to download or buy. The call-to-action (CTA) should be a natural extension:
   - *Example CTA:* *"If you want the clean single-column .docx template and the full audit checklist we use, you can grab the free Fresher Job Application Kit from the link in our bio/comments."*

## 3. Platform Distribution Guidelines

### A. LinkedIn (Primary B2B / Professional Channel)
- **Primary Formats:** Text-only diagnostic breakdowns (150–250 words) and multi-page PDF Document Carousels (6–8 slides).
- **Tone:** Objective, analytical, practical, mentor-like.
- **Link Placement:** In the pinned first comment or direct profile featured section to avoid algorithmic link penalties.

### B. Reddit (`r/developersIndia`, `r/Indian_Academia`)
- **Rules of Engagement:** Strict non-promotional policy. Never drop a raw download link in a top-level post.
- **Methodology:** Post exhaustive, self-contained educational guides answering frequent community questions (e.g., *"A complete guide to why your Canva resume is getting ignored by off-campus recruiters"*).
- **Link Protocol:** If users in the comments explicitly ask: *"Do you have a template for this?"*, reply with a direct, zero-tracking link to the free repository.

### C. YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels (Short-Form Video)
- **Format:** 45–60 second rapid-fire breakdowns using screen recordings of real vs. broken resume formatting.
- **Visuals:** High-contrast text highlights, red X on bad elements (photo, declaration, skill bars), green check on clean layout.
- **Tone:** Fast-paced, direct, no filler intros.

### D. Search-Friendly Web & Markdown Guides
- **Format:** In-depth, evergreen guides published on a lightweight GitHub Pages or Markdown site.
- **Focus Queries:** *"Indian fresher resume format docx"*, *"How to optimize Naukri profile for freshers"*, *"What to remove from engineering resume"*.

## 5. Detailed Content Outlines & Draft Scripts

### Post 1 (LinkedIn Carousel): "5 Things to Delete from Your Fresher Resume"
- **Slide 1 (Hook):** If your resume still has a "Declaration" at the bottom, your college placement cell gave you a 1990s biodata template. Here are 5 things hurting your off-campus applications.
- **Slide 2 (Father's Name & Marital Status):** Why tech recruiters don't need your family genealogy. What to replace it with: Clean GitHub & LinkedIn links.
- **Slide 3 (The Formal Declaration):** *"I hereby declare..."* Recruiters know you're not lying under oath; it wastes 3 lines of prime vertical real estate.
- **Slide 4 (Your Photograph):** Off-campus tech hiring in India does not require headshots. Photos frequently confuse image scrapers and introduce unconscious bias.
- **Slide 5 (Skill Percentage Bars):** *"Java: 85%"* — 85% of what? Did you write the JVM? Replace subjective rating bars with projects demonstrating the skill.
- **Slide 6 (Full Street Address):** House number, lane, and landmark are irrelevant and pose a privacy risk. Use: `City, State` only.
- **Slide 7 (Summary & Resource):** Clean, 1-page single-column layout wins. Grab the free single-column .docx template in the comments.

---

### Post 2 (Reddit / r/developersIndia): "The Mechanics of Naukri Profile Ranking"
- **Opening:** *"Most freshers treat Naukri like a lottery ticket: upload a resume once, apply to 50 jobs a day, and wonder why they get 0 calls. Here is how the recruiter side (Resdex) actually operates."*
- **Key Point 1: The Recency Filter:** Recruiters filter candidate pools by *"Active within 15 days"*. Making a 30-second edit to your profile headline or summary resets your active timestamp.
- **Key Point 2: The Key Skills Field:** Resdex searches primarily query the Key Skills tags, NOT the body of your uploaded PDF. If a skill isn't in your 25 tags, you don't show up in search queries.
- **Key Point 3: The Resume Headline:** Formula: `[Degree + Batch] | [Core Tech Stack] | [Target Role]`.
- **Closing:** Practical tips on avoiding fake consultancy calls asking for interview fees.

## 6. Community Respect & Anti-Spam Protocols

1. **Zero Link Blasting:** Never drop promotional links in comments of unrelated posts.
2. **Value First:** If participating in Reddit discussions, provide complete, comprehensive answers directly in the forum without gating knowledge behind a click.
3. **Respect Moderators:** Always adhere to subreddit rules regarding self-promotion days (e.g., Saturday showcase threads).
4. **Tone of Empathy:** Never belittle or shame freshers for making mistakes. Recognize that colleges and influencers have given them bad advice.
