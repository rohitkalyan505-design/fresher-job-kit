```markdown
If your resume still ends with a "Declaration" paragraph and your Father's Name, your college placement cell gave you a 1990s biodata template.

In off-campus tech and corporate hiring in 2026, recruiters spend roughly 6 to 10 seconds on an initial scan. Every single line on your 1-page resume is high-stakes real estate.

Yet, thousands of Indian freshers waste 20% to 30% of their resume on archaic information that hiring managers never look at.

Here are 5 things you should delete from your fresher resume today—and what to put instead:

1. Father’s Name & Family Biodata
Why it hurts: Recruiters are evaluating your engineering ability, not your genealogy. In private tech hiring, family details signal an outdated mindset and waste 2–3 valuable lines.
Exception: Statutory government job forms (UPSC/SSC) or formal PSU background check applications where mandated by law.
What to put instead: Your live GitHub profile link, clean LinkedIn URL, and portfolio website.

2. The Formal "Declaration" Block
Why it hurts: "I hereby declare that the details above are true to the best of my knowledge..." + Date + Place + Signature block wastes 4–5 lines. Submitting a job application legally implies truthfulness. Zero recruiters read declarations.
What to put instead: An extra project impact bullet or a verified technical certification.

3. Candidate Photograph / Headshot
Why it hurts: In Indian tech off-campus hiring, photos add zero evaluation value. Worse, they can confuse optical text scrapers and introduce unconscious bias.
Exception: Aviation, hospitality, acting/media, or European CV standards (e.g. Germany/France) where photos are conventional.
What to put instead: A clean text-only contact header: Full Name, Target Title, City & State, Phone (+91), and Email.

4. Subjective Skill Rating Bars ("Java: 85%")
Why it hurts: Canva templates love skill bars and star ratings. But technical interviewers find them meaningless: 85% of what? Did you invent the JVM? Rating yourself invites brutal interview grilling on compiler internals.
What to put instead: Categorized skill tags (Languages, Frameworks, Developer Tools, Core CS Fundamentals). Let your actual project bullets prove your competence.

5. Full Street / House Door Address
Why it hurts: Putting your house number, street name, and local landmark creates an unnecessary privacy risk when uploading resumes across dozens of job portals. Recruiters only care about your city and relocation flexibility.
What to put instead: Just City, State (e.g., Bengaluru, Karnataka or Hyderabad, Telangana).

---

THE 1-PAGE PAYOFF:
When you eliminate these 5 items, you immediately reclaim 8 to 12 lines of vertical space. 

Use that space to showcase:
✓ 2–3 real technical projects with GitHub & demo links
✓ Action verbs (Engineered, Architected, Optimized)
✓ Concrete scope (Test coverage %, latency reduction, query indexing, dataset rows)
```
