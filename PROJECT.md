## 1. Executive Business Mission

The primary goal of this business is to generate sustainable digital-product revenue by discovering real, painful customer problems in high-friction workflows and engineering high-utility digital solutions that solve those problems better, faster, and more affordably than alternatives.

We reject the "make an ebook and push it on strangers" model. We operate on a lean, evidence-based iteration loop:

```
REAL CUSTOMER PROBLEM
       ↓
DEMAND EVIDENCE (Gather raw complaints, observed frictions, and search intent)
       ↓
VALIDATE PROBLEM (Confirm depth and recurring frequency of pain)
       ↓
IDENTIFY GAPS (Analyze where existing free and paid tools fail users)
       ↓
BUILD USEFUL FREE MVP (Zero-cost, high-utility baseline that solves the core bottleneck)
       ↓
DISTRIBUTE VIA VALUABLE CONTENT (Teach, diagnose, and solve openly without clickbait)
       ↓
OBSERVE USER BEHAVIOR & REQUESTS (Track downloads, issues, unprompted feature requests)
       ↓
ITERATE & REFINE (Tighten product based on real friction points)
       ↓
CREATE PAID EXPANDED VERSION (Only if and when willingness to pay is demonstrated)
       ↓
SELL & MEASURE REVENUE
       ↓
REPEAT / SCALE
```

## 2. Current Market Experiment

- **Target Audience:** Indian college students (final/pre-final year) and fresh graduates (freshers) seeking entry-level software, tech, and corporate roles or internships.
- **Problem Domain:** High job-application rejection rates, ATS parsing errors, generic resumes, lack of job-description (JD) keyword tailoring, ineffective Naukri.com profile setups, and disorganized application tracking.
- **Core Working Title:** **Fresher Job Application Kit (Free MVP)**.

## 3. Strict Operational & Financial Boundaries (₹0 Policy)

1. **₹0 Upfront Spending Policy:**
   - No paid ad campaigns (Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads).
   - No paid software licenses or SaaS subscriptions when free tools or local scripts suffice.
   - No paid contractors, agencies, or outsourced labor.
   - All tools, templates, and runtime environments must be operable by the end-user using free software (e.g., Google Docs/Sheets, LibreOffice, free PDF viewers).
2. **Founder Approval Protocol:**
   - All public releases, customer-facing content, product artifacts, pricing experiments, and potential expenditures require explicit review and sign-off by Rohit (Founder).
3. **Epistemological Discipline:**
   - Always differentiate between **FACT**, **EVIDENCE**, **INFERENCE**, and **HYPOTHESIS**.
   - Never assert unproven assumptions (e.g., "users will pay ₹199" is an unvalidated hypothesis, not a fact).
   - No fabricated metrics, vanity numbers, fake testimonials, or artificial engagement.
4. **Product Integrity:**
   - No claims guaranteeing job placement or interview callbacks.
   - No false claims that "ATS algorithms reject 99% of candidates automatically" or that an arbitrary "ATS score" guarantees success.
   - Example resume bullets must be clearly flagged as synthetic illustrations, never fabricating credentials or real-world company experiences for candidates.
