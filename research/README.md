# Research Archive & Source Data

This directory houses raw research logs, community interview transcripts, competitor teardowns, and citation records.

## Sub-areas
- `community_threads/`: Raw dumps and key takeaways from Reddit (`r/developersIndia`), Quora, and LinkedIn forums.
- `competitor_analysis/`: Feature and pricing teardowns of existing free and paid tools (Canva, Overleaf, Naukri FastForward, Zety).
- `ats_mechanics/`: Technical documentation on ATS parsing engines (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, Darwinbox) and text extraction benchmarks.
